## Method notes / what could not be fully verified
- Sitemap index is valid and lists 7 child sitemaps (post, page, category, author, uc, uc_math, uc_time). The site is on WordPress + Yoast SEO.
- `page-sitemap.xml` (the file holding the real calculator pages) is larger than one fetch can return; it was cut off after 231 `<loc>` entries ending at gravel-driveway-calculator. The curated inventory below therefore comes from the site's own category hierarchy via `https://www.inchcalculator.com/sitemap/` plus the 40 category/subcategory pages cited in source_urls.
- `uc_sitemap.xml` and `uc_math_sitemap.xml` are huge programmatic files (pairwise unit pairs; "X as a fraction" / "X as a percent" answer pages). They were sampled, not enumerated.
- Counted as TOOLS: anything with an interactive input (calculator, converter, estimator, countdown, timer, clock).
- Excluded as NOT tools: blog posts and articles, "Additional Resources" article links, cost/price guides (there is a separate large "Cost Calculators & Price Guides" stack reached via /cost-guides/ with 15 subcategories — these are editorial cost guides such as /cost-to-install-air-conditioning/, not interactive tools; I sampled them on the construction, plumbing-hvac, roofing, siding and wall-ceiling pages but did not enumerate them), marketing/lead-gen pages (e.g. /get-flooring-estimates/, /local-roofing-contractor/), legal and company pages, category index pages.
- Reference tables/charts were not counted as tools; they are listed under "Uncertain / non-tool reference pages" at the end.
