# Organic growth measurement plan

## Goal and baseline

The owner's goal of **2,000,000 Google Search impressions in the first month after public
launch is a stretch target, not a forecast or promise**. No Search Console baseline, indexation
history, backlink baseline, or query performance is available in this repository. Calculator page
count and competitor page counts do not predict impressions.

Do not begin the launch-month clock until the owner has selected the production domain, verified
it in Search Console, and recorded the public-launch date. No domain or DNS change is authorized
by this plan.

## Data to record

In Search Console, export date-stamped page/query data at least weekly for the first month and
monthly thereafter:

- Indexed quality calculator and guide URLs versus submitted URLs.
- Impressions, clicks, CTR, and average position by page and query.
- New pages discovered, crawled, indexed, excluded, and their exclusion reasons.
- Queries with impressions but low CTR, segmented by page and position before changing titles.
- Pages with impressions but low positions, grouped by distinct intent and category.
- Branded versus non-branded queries and country/device segmentation.
- Clicks and conversions from organic landing pages only if analytics is configured with a
  documented consent/privacy approach.
- Relevant new referring domains, with the linking URL, target URL, and verification date.

Keep Search Console data separate from third-party estimates and from competitor coverage.
Never label estimated search volume or competitor traffic as Calculate Daily impressions.

## Review cadence and decisions

1. **Before launch:** record the verified indexable URL set, production canonical host, sitemap
   submission, Search Console verification, and analytics configuration. Keep preview hosts
   noindex and out of the production sitemap.
2. **Weekly for the first month:** record impressions, clicks, CTR, queries, position, and
   indexation by page. Check that only reviewed, useful pages are indexed.
3. **At day 28–35:** compare actual impressions with the 2,000,000 stretch target; report the
   absolute gap and the measured change from the first Search Console week. Do not backfill
   missing data or extrapolate a short interval as a guarantee.
4. **Monthly afterward:** prioritize page improvements from real query/page data, merge
   overlapping content, and review technical crawl/indexation issues before adding low-evidence
   URLs.
5. **Backlinks:** record only links confirmed at their published source. Outreach is manual,
   relevant, and non-incentivized; no paid links, automated comments, mass exchanges, or fake
   directories.

## Current measurement gaps

- Search Console is not configured because the production domain is not configured.
- No real search impressions, clicks, CTR, positions, or indexation measurements are supplied.
- No verified backlink acquisition dataset is supplied.
- `docs/keyword-intelligence/` contains research candidates and competitor coverage, not
  first-party demand metrics. Its demand fields do not supply Search Console evidence or a
  traffic forecast.
